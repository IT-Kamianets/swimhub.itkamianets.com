Training Plans

Use simple pool sessions for different levels. Rest when needed and choose the plan that matches your current comfort.

looks_one

First pool session

Beginner 30 minutes

Goal: Build comfort in the water

Warm-up: 5 minutes easy swim

  • 4 x 25m freestyle
  • 4 x 25m kickboard
  • 2 x 25m easy swim

Cool down: 5 minutes slow swim

Rest between each lap and focus on breathing.

looks_two

Steady technique session

Intermediate 45 minutes

Goal: Improve stroke rhythm and endurance

Warm-up: 200m easy swim with mixed strokes

  • 6 x 50m freestyle
  • 4 x 25m backstroke
  • 4 x 25m drill work

Cool down: 100m relaxed swim

Keep the same pace from the first repeat to the last.

looks_3

Endurance builder

Advanced 60 minutes

Goal: Train stamina and pace control

Warm-up: 400m easy swim plus 4 x 25m build

  • 8 x 100m freestyle
  • 4 x 50m kick
  • 4 x 50m strong finish

Cool down: 200m slow mixed swim

Use consistent rest intervals and track split times.
