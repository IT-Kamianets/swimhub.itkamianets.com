Glossary

A simple dictionary of common swimming terms for beginners.

Pool

Lap

One length of the pool.

Lane

A marked swimming path in the pool.

Open water

Swimming in lakes, rivers, or the sea.

Technique

Stroke

A swimming style or movement pattern.

Turn

Changing direction at the wall.

Training

Split time

The time for one part of a swim.

Drill

A focused exercise for improving one skill.

Equipment

Kickboard

A floating board used for kick practice.

Competition

Relay

A team race where swimmers take turns.

Medley

A race or set using multiple strokes.
